03

Customer experience

Customers move from discovery to a coordinated booking, then into tracking, account management, billing, subscriptions, and support.

Discover

Home, services, subscriptions, yearly contracts, offers, and login.

  • Service discovery
  • Plan comparison
  • Account entry

Book and track

A single shared slot coordinates multiple requested services.

  • Select services
  • Location and slot
  • Review, payment, confirmation
  • Live tracking

Manage

The customer workspace keeps the delivery relationship organized.

  • Orders and addresses
  • Subscriptions and membership
  • Wallet and bills
  • Support and contracts

04

Vendor experience

Vendors convert available demand into managed delivery operations through orders, fleet, drivers, earnings, complaints, and alerts.

Operate

Dashboard, available orders, active delivery, and earnings.

  • Review incoming orders
  • Select vehicle and driver
  • Accept delivery

Deliver

The order state advances from acceptance through OTP-verified completion.

  • En route
  • Arrived
  • Delivery OTP
  • Delivered

Manage fleet

Operational records support availability and compliance.

  • Services
  • Vehicles
  • Drivers
  • Earnings, complaints, notifications

05

Admin experience

Admins operate the platform through a denser command center with oversight, configuration, approval, finance, and exception handling.

Monitor

KPIs, live operations, order queues, reports, finance, and payments.

  • Operational overview
  • Live vehicle stream
  • Revenue and reconciliation

Control

Admin manages the entities and rules that make delivery possible.

  • Vendors and onboarding
  • Vehicles and drivers
  • Services and pricing
  • Subscriptions and coupons

Resolve

Exceptions become explicit review and approval workflows.

  • Complaints and enquiries
  • Notifications and alerts
  • Approve, reject, suspend
  • Order detail investigation

06

Cross-role domain flow

The visual system follows the actual operational handoff: customer request, vendor fulfillment, admin oversight, and customer confirmation.

sequenceDiagram participant Customer participant Platform participant Vendor participant Admin Customer->>Platform: Create booking Platform->>Vendor: Broadcast available order Platform->>Admin: Add order to operations queue Vendor->>Platform: Select vehicle and driver Vendor->>Platform: Accept order Platform-->>Customer: Order accepted and tracking enabled Vendor->>Platform: Update en route / arrived Platform-->>Customer: Live status update Vendor->>Platform: Verify delivery OTP Platform->>Customer: Mark delivered and create invoice Platform->>Admin: Update finance and operational metrics Customer->>Platform: Rate, subscribe, or request support Admin->>Platform: Resolve exceptions and manage configuration
