About Urban Tanker

Chennai’s water logistics, made visible.

Urban Tanker connects homes, apartments, hotels, industries and construction sites with verified water and sewage tanker partners—bringing clear pricing, predictable arrival times and accountable delivery to every order.

Our company

A dependable operating layer for essential water services.

We are building a technology-led tanker network for Chennai. Customers can book verified vehicles, understand the full charge before paying, follow their tanker live and confirm delivery securely with an OTP.

For partners, Urban Tanker creates organised demand, clear job information and a professional digital workflow. For businesses, we provide repeatable schedules, invoices and one place to manage multiple delivery sites.
